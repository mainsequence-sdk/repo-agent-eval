The evidence analysis should center **Verify effective access**: Confirm the intended effective permission while detecting conflicts and preserving unrelated access.

Local configuration presence and schema validation support only a declaration-level conclusion. They do not prove that the intended identity was selected, that an owned operation completed, or that runtime behavior is correct. The governing boundary is: resolve stable public principals and actual resource identities, choose least privilege, separate team membership from grants, reconcile idempotently, and preserve unrelated access.

The unsupported column should explicitly reject live success or failure claims that require absent state. The response must preserve ambiguity instead of inventing uids, credentials, logs, releases, runs, or outputs.

The ordered observation column should request the narrowest relevant chain: resolved principal/resource identities, requested and effective permissions, preserved grants, and redacted named-configuration state. It should stop when an earlier identity, authorization, or lifecycle prerequisite fails and should preserve unrelated state.
